## §0 — Maintainer rulings, 2026-09-05. Do not re-litigate these.

### September 9 follow-up rulings

Aedis's direct instructions for the Japan pilot update the execution of the earlier
rules, not their raw-source or one-roster/one-vote safeguards:

- Initial reference placement includes **stats and price**. Classic original prices
  may change; subsequent fitting may adjust stats and price jointly on the existing
  permitted grids. A near-zero formula delta is not gameplay validation.
- Implement source-relative versus fitted-target separation; freeze pilot calibration
  inputs; expose counterpart/analogue/extrapolation status and disagreement; validate
  withheld references; and compare representative counter/tier/TTK relationships before
  recommending live changes. These are distinct checks, not one numeric acceptance score.
- MCVs and harvesters are collection-only/manual-review subjects. Harvesters need their
  separate formula. Neither may silently enter automatic combat-unit fitting.
- Extract RA3, Dune II and Emperor reference evidence; obtain versioned Spice Wars data.
  Exact DTA re-extraction awaits the files Aedis will provide. Unsupported damage channels
  and unresolved source dependencies must remain visible and must not become complete
  numeric evidence merely because a column is present.

Instruction timestamps, implementation boundaries and pending evidence are recorded in
[the September 9 ruling record](../balance/review/AEDIS_OVERNIGHT_RULINGS_20260909.md).
These instructions do not sign off anchors or authorize merging PRs.

| R16 | **A cell an attack CANNOT REACH is `n/a`, never 100.** `WeaponInfo.ValidTargets` and `Warhead.ValidTargets` both default to `new("Ground","Water")` (WeaponInfo.cs:116, Warhead.cs:30), so a weapon that never mentions targeting cannot hit air at all — **366 of Combined Arms' 695 weapons**. | R17 | **Each source matrix is normalised to a usage-weighted GEOMETRIC mean of 100, inside Cameo's own 20:1 window.** §12.0 rule 4's `[10,200]` band is unit-free because it is relative to the matrix's own centre, which is what makes DTA (centre ~590) and OpenRA TD (~61) comparable. ⛔ **Zeros are ABSENCES: excluded from the centre and from the clamp.** Treating a zero as `centre × 0.10` makes the window a runaway — measured on Mental Omega (20.8% zeros) the centre fell 32.3 → 13.5 → 4.1 → 0.2 and never settled, flattening the matrix. Every source above ~13% zeros did it; below ~8% it converged, which is why the flaw stayed invisible until the YR mods were added. |
| R18 | ⚠ **"Geometric mean is 100 and the range is [10,200]" IS NOT A CHECK.** It holds *by construction*, because the matrix is normalised by the very centre being tested — it passed on all six degenerate sources. The independent quantity is the source's own **positive median**: a matrix has collapsed when its typical working value no longer fits inside its own window. `warhead_matrix.py --check`. |

| R21 | **A reference warhead's family is assigned BY HAND from the compression groups, never by matching its name.** Name matching failed three times in one session and the last one is the clearest: `BazAP` was classified as Tesla because `Ba`**`zAP`** contains "zap"; `ChronoBeam` and `LocomotorBeam` matched "Laser" and are mind-control effects with no damage profile. | R22 | **One warhead per weapon on the reference side too.** 116 of Combined Arms' 466 armed weapons carry more than one damage warhead; entering each as a separate weapon put one weapon in three groups. That count came from a scan that treated every warhead node as a weapon, and picking by name reported false immunities. Fold, do not pick. |
| R23 | **A weapon's profile is the SUM of its warheads, not its biggest one.** The maintainer queried four groups in the 2026-09-21 review — *"why is light immune?"*, *"how is the damage so low against None?"*, *"none immune is a bug"*, *"anti light only at 18 while the rest is all at 176?"* — and all four were one measurement defect. OpenRA fires **every** warhead on every hit, and CA's extra warheads are routinely COMPLEMENTARY: `FireballLauncher` states `Light: 0` on `@1Dam` and `Light: 50` on `@2Dam`; `JDAM` and `MaverickSU` put their whole anti-infantry half on `@2Dam`; `ApocRadBeamWeapon`'s `@1Dam` is an infantry-only `HealthPercentageDamage` rider. So fold by effective damage — `versus(A) = 100 x SUM(damage_w x versus_w(A)) / D`, `D = SUM(damage_w)`, a warhead that cannot reach `A` contributing 0 to the numerator and still counting in `D`, which keeps the row RATIOS equal to real effective-damage ratios. ⚠ A per-armour denominator (a plain weighted average) flattens exactly the weapons that split their target sets. ⚠ `HealthPercentageDamage` is a different currency (`HP x Damage/100 x Versus/100`) and folds only with its own kind. Implemented as `warhead_matrix.fold_by_weapon`. |
| R24 | **A percentage warhead is priced through the typical unit it hits.** Maintainer: *"percentage warheads need to be regarded as a flat damage against the average unit of that armor type, so 300% against the average of 50k HP light armor means it's 150k damage against light"*. `HealthPercentageDamage` resolves as `HP x Damage/100 x Versus/100`, so its damage against class `A` is `avgHP(A) x Damage/100` — armour-DEPENDENT, which is exactly the property that makes a percentage weapon relatively better against heavy classes. `avgHP` is the GEOMETRIC mean HP of the buildable actors declaring that armour (CA: None 6,336 · Light 18,774 · Aircraft 23,524 · Concrete 48,973 · Heavy 52,994 · Wood 89,166). Excluding percentage warheads instead — the first attempt — made a sniper whose entire anti-infantry punch is a 300%-of-HP warhead read as a mild anti-vehicle gun. ⚠ Overkill is deliberately NOT modelled, on the maintainer's instruction; the flat side is not capped either, so capping one would bias the comparison rather than fix it. |
| R25 | **Reachability and coverage are resolved per ARMOUR CLASS, not per GND/AIR macro.** R16's macro mask is enough to decide `n/a`, and NOT enough to fold: 380mm's second warhead deals 70,000 with `ValidTargets: Infantry`, and summing that into Heavy and Wood — both "ground" — turns a siege gun into a tank killer. So a warhead reaches armour `A` only if some buildable actor declaring `A` carries a matching `Targetable` type, and it contributes in proportion to the FRACTION of that class it can hit. Coverage is not optional: 3 of Combined Arms' 63 Light-armour actors are cyborgs declaring `Infantry`, so without it a sniper's infantry-only rider reached the Light column at full strength and put `Light` ABOVE `None` on a sniper. |
| R26 | **The reference map is ONE ROW PER CAMEO WARHEAD.** Maintainer: *"everything that shares the same cameo warhead type is only listed once in one list ... one group per cameo warhead mapping"*. `compress_warheads.py` answers what distinct behaviours a mod SHIPS (126 groups for CA); `family_matrix.py` answers what each of OUR warheads must BE (32 live families), collapsing the groups onto their assigned family by usage-weighted geometric mean. A per-weapon override beats its group, so a group contributes only the weapons that stayed with it and its usage is split across its destinations. |
| R27 | **An AA armament is not a separate weapon — fold the pair at ACTOR level.** Cameo's shipped dual-armament law is *"AA split = one weapon"*, the air side earning a 1.5x range bonus and anti-air vehicles +100% firepower — a MODIFIER layer, not a second warhead. Combined Arms splits them, and counting the halves separately invented a family: `MissileAA` was the third-largest in the map at 43 weapons, of which **39 were the AA half of a dual-purpose unit** (Flak: 7 of 9; Laser: 5 of 6). ⚠ Only an UNAMBIGUOUS pair folds — exactly one ground and one air armament on the actor. The Venom's pairing is obvious; the IFV carries thirty ground weapons and four AA ones and choosing a partner there would be a guess. The merged magnitude is the USAGE-WEIGHTED GEOMETRIC MEAN of the two, never a sum (they never fire at the same target), and the pair takes its GROUND half's family. Result: MissileAA 33 air-only weapons, Flak 3, and every dual-purpose ground weapon stops reporting `n/a` against aircraft it can plainly shoot down. |
| R28 | **No `LaserAA` and no `LaserAir`.** Both were considered and rejected on measurement. A dual-purpose laser is already expressed by the R27 modifier layer, so encoding it in the Versus table as well would double-count it; and a genuinely AA-ONLY laser has one candidate in the whole corpus (`MantisLaser`; in Cameo, the Cabal Obelisk of Darkness), which does not earn a warhead. ⭐ Generalises: a dual-purpose family per delivery is minted only where a reference mod shows a real, distinct profile — never for symmetry. |
| R29 | **A family's profile pools its member WEAPONS' rows, never their groups' profiles.** A group is a REVIEW device (120 rows a human can read instead of 550), and a per-weapon override routinely sends one member somewhere else. Pooling group shapes drags the whole group along with that one weapon: `MissileAA` came out with a full ground profile (Heavy 105.6, Light 182.7) on the strength of ONE of its 33 members sitting in a ground-capable group, when every other member is air-only and `n/a` there. |
| R30 | **Cameo votes as one mod among the twenty, and uniqueness is resolved only after all twenty are in.** Extends R20 from the armour layer to the warhead layer. Separation applies on COLLISION only, along the axis where the sources disagree most — so today's 8 near-identical CA pairs (`Laser`/`Tesla` 0.084, `Flame`/`Plasma` 0.088, `Bullet`/`Arrow` 0.105 …) are recorded, not acted on: CA is one vote of twenty and a collision on its data alone may not survive averaging. |
| R31 | **NO CAMEO WARHEAD IS AIR-ONLY — the landed-aircraft rule.** Maintainer: *"MissileAA should still be able to damage ground targets but have the highest versus value against air. What if the aircraft lands? Then it counts as ground, and when the missile is still in flight and the aircraft lands it should of course still deal damage."* The MEASUREMENT stays honest — CA's SAM sites really do state no ground opinion, and writing 100 there is the Aircraft-148 defect pointed the other way — but a Cameo warhead has to fire at whatever it is aimed at. So an air-only family's ground rows are DERIVED from the same DELIVERY's measured warheads (a SAM is a missile) and scaled by `AA_GROUND_RATIO` (0.5) of the measured air value, so the air row stays the peak. Those cells are marked `derived_ground` and shown differently on the map. ⚠ A FALLBACK, not a law: most reference mods DO give their flak cannons and SAM sites real ground rows, so these cells should be replaced by measurement once the other nineteen sources are averaged in. |
| R32 | **Pair armaments by DISTINCT PROFILE, not by distinct name.** OpenRA gives one logical weapon several armament entries for mechanical reasons — firing ports, left/right mounts, garrison slots. The Yak carries `ChainGun.Yak.L` and `ChainGun.Yak.R`, byte-identical profiles on two wing mounts, and counting them as two ground weapons made the actor look ambiguous and blocked the R27 fold of its obvious `ChainGun.Yak.AA`. Counting distinct profiles took Combined Arms from 17 folded pairs to 29. ⚠ And one ground weapon can adopt SEVERAL air variants (`HellfireAG` pairs with both `HellfireAA` and `HellfireAA.Cryo`): merge once per GROUND weapon, or the result carries two rows with the same weapon key and every consumer keyed by weapon name silently collapses them. |
| R33 | **A source's armours are ANCHORED to positions on our ladders, then LOG-LINEARLY interpolated.** Implemented as `tools/reference/armor_interpolate.py`. An anchor pins a source armour at an index on one of our four ladders (INF 3 rungs, VEH 5, BLD 3, AIR 4) — exactly (`combined_arms.None` IS our `None`), fractionally (`combined_arms.Light` at VEH 0.5, between Scout and Light), flat across a whole ladder (`combined_arms.Aircraft`, the only source with a real air tag), or excluded (walls, terrain, projectile-interception classes). Unpinned rungs are filled in LOG space, because these are multipliers and the step between rungs is a ratio. ⚠ A ladder with ONE anchor fills only its own rung: a single point carries no slope, and inventing the rest would invent the source's opinion — OpenRA Red Alert genuinely has no second infantry rung and so has no view on Flak. ⚠ EXTRAPOLATION IS CLAMPED to the §12.0 window [10, 200]: the transcribed Dune 2000 table pins its building ladder only at Steel and Concrete, and running that slope down to Wood produced 250. ⭐ `Heroic` is never interpolated — §12.0b makes it the derived column `Plate x Scout / peak`, recomputed after the ladders are filled. |
| R34 | **SHAPE and LEVEL are averaged separately, because the sources do not all vote on every rung.** A plain per-cell mean compares populations of different size and invents an inversion no source contains: `None` had 18 voters and `Flak` only 9, the nine extra `None`-only voters were the low sources (Tiberian Sun 81, Tiberian Dawn 89, Dune 2000 91), and the average came out `None` 109.4 BELOW `Flak` 119.9 — while **8 of the 9 sources with an opinion on both rungs put them the right way round**. §12.0d says a ladder can never invert, so that was a broken measurement, not a finding. ⭐ A source that pinned TWO OR MORE rungs of a ladder votes on that ladder's SHAPE (its own rungs normalised to a geometric mean of 1, which makes sources with different coverage comparable); every source that pinned ANY rung votes on the ladder's LEVEL; the answer is shape x level. Nobody's data is discarded and nobody's absence distorts a rung. All four ladders then come out monotonic from 18 independent sources: INF 112/111/98, VEH 134/117/100/88/78, BLD 106/86/67, AIR 122/116/109/103. |
| R35 | **RESOLVED — a source's vote on a ladder is weighted by how much of that ladder it really has.** An armour that anchors two ladders contributes ONE measured number to both, and that number is dominated by whichever macro type wears the tag most. Combined Arms is the worked example: its `Heavy` anchors our Plate infantry rung AND the top of the vehicle ladder, but of its **101 wearers, 98 are vehicles and 3 are cyborgs** (ENLI/REAP/RMBC — genuinely CA's heaviest infantry, but targeted as `cyborg`+`vehicle`, never `infantry`), so Plate was being set by a number describing heavy tanks. CA was the only source of eighteen to invert its own infantry ladder. ⭐ Fix: weight each source's SHAPE vote on a ladder by the geometric mean of its anchors' wearer share for that ladder's macro type. Nothing is discarded — CA still speaks about infantry, just quietly. The infantry ladder went from 112.1/110.5/98.3 to **112.8/108.7/99.3**, and all four ladders are monotonic. A source with no actor census (the INI dialects, the transcribed table) speaks at full strength: its armour names are already per-macro-type. |
| R36 | **A cyborg is infantry x vehicle and votes HALF on each ladder** (maintainer's ruling, 2026-09-21). DESIGN.md's *Cyborg dual-armor rule* already says why: a CABAL cyborg keeps its infantry-class `Armor` and adds a vehicle `Armor@<role>`, *"so cyborgs count as both infantry and vehicles for weapon Versus tables"*, and the two rows are **AVERAGED, not multiplied** (`AreaDamageWarhead.MultiArmorCombination: Average` — `Plate` 88 with `Superheavy` 10 resolves to 49, not 8). The same pattern covers Future Tech droids and Schwarzer Mond. So in the wearer census a cyborg counts 0.5 toward INF and 0.5 toward VEH, never 1.0 to either. ⚠ This is also why Crystallized Nexus looked like it had an overlapping `Vehicle` tag: it runs the same two-layer scheme, a macro tag multiplied with a weight class. |
| R37 | **Cameo's own vote needs no compression — its template NAME is the family.** `compress_warheads` looked the source id up in `OPENRA_SOURCES`, which lists the REFERENCE mods only, so Cameo's groups came out delivery `Unknown` / band `unused` while R20 and R30 both give it a vote. `cameo_resolved` is fixed by adding this repository's own ruleset to the lookup (174 groups, 173 with a real delivery). The TEMPLATE view needs no fix at all: a `^Warhead_*` template is abstract and has no `Projectile:`, but `^Warhead_Arrow_Heavy` IS the Arrow family by definition. ⚠ That is not a breach of R21's ban on name-matching: R21 forbids inferring a REFERENCE mod's weapon class from its name, where the names lie; these are our own names, which are the vocabulary itself. All 150 templates parse into **50 families** x {Light, Medium, Heavy, Super}, against the 32 Combined Arms needs — the gap is the consolidation target. |
| R38 | **RESOLVED — the Westwood INI dialects carry delivery and element in a different shape, and it is extractable.** `compress_warheads` keys on `Projectile:` and `DamageTypes:`; the INI mods have neither, and the obvious substitute is a trap — Mental Omega's 1,117 weapons name projectiles `InvisibleWork`, `InvisibleHigh`, `CannonInviso`, which are trajectory and rendering variants, not weapon classes. Nine sources compressed to nothing but `Unknown`. ⭐ The real signals sit in three places and every token was COUNTED in the files rather than taken from documentation: **weapon flags** `IsLaser` (MO 145, TI 18), `IsBigLaser`, `IsElectricBolt` (MO 42), `IsRailgun` (TI 25), `IsDetachedRailgun` (MO 93), `IsRadBeam`, `Lobber`; **the [Projectile] section's behaviour** `ROT` (it steers, so it is a missile), `Arcing` (ballistic shell), `Inviso` (MO 121, hits instantly), `Vertical` (a bomb); **warhead flags** `Fire`, `Tiberium` (MO 48, TI 50), `EMEffect`, `Bullets` (MO 71), `Radiation`, `Sonic`, `Temporal`, plus `InfDeath`. ⚠ `InfDeath` IS A DEATH ANIMATION, the same trap as OpenRA's `*Death` tokens, so only the codes unambiguous across TS and RA2 are used — 3 burn, 4 electrocute, 6/7 chemical — and explicit flags always win. The band comes from which registry (`InfantryTypes`/`VehicleTypes`/`AircraftTypes`/`BuildingTypes`) the firing actor is listed in. Result: **17 of 20 sources compress, 1,202 groups across the corpus** (Mental Omega 17 -> 130, Rise of the East 13 -> 105, Red Resurrection 14 -> 104, DTA 9 -> 35). The three that do not are by construction: `cameo` needs no compression (R37), `cameo_resolved` is at 173 of 174, and `d2k_mod` is a transcribed table with no actors, so it votes on the armour ladder only. |
| R39 | **MEASURED, AND IT FAILS — family propagation by the delivery/element/band triple is 19% accurate, and the GROUP, not the method, is why.** R38 left 512 of 1,059 groups needing a ruling and the other 547 as inherited proposals; nothing had checked whether an inherited proposal was ever right. It is checkable for free: a Cameo weapon inheriting `^Warhead_<Family>_<Level>` states its family in the yaml (R37), and **904 (904 when measured 2026-09-22; **1,291** on master `afb66c9b5` 2026-09-26; **1,512** on `devin/nova/w7-packs` post-merge / **1,345** on the 2026-09-27 DAWN stack merge; **1,566** on the #534+#516 merged tree / **1,567** on 2026-10-02 master — `doc_claims: cameo_family_labelled_weapons`) concrete weapons do**, so compressing Cameo's own weapons and asking the propagation to recover Cameo's own answers is a labelled test that needed no maintainer time. ## §3 — Traps already paid for. Do not rediscover these.

1. **`git grep` skips several of our weapons yaml as binary** (non-UTF-8 bytes), and **`miniyaml`
   silently under-parses the same files.** For any presence check use
   `git show <rev>:<file> | grep -a`. This nearly deleted 30 live weapon nodes.
2. **A mod's loose `rulesmd.ini` can be vanilla Yuri's Revenge byte-for-byte** — Mental Omega's is
   (md5 `cf7eb658327aff1fe7e6c4e7400eb87f`). Check every extraction against that hash.
3. **`extract_mix_ini.py` sniffs only the first 4096 bytes**, so a rules file with a comment banner
   reports "0 INI blobs found". Judge blobs by full content.
4. **`Versus` is an empty-valued node** — see A5.
5. **A claim about the corpus is not a claim about the world.** "MO and CnC Reloaded are not
   recoverable" was recorded as fact in `REFERENCE_PIPELINE_HANDOFF.md` §1.3; both were on disk.
